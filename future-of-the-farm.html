Future of the Farm

Our 27 acres are currently in the middle of an exciting transition as we work to design a more resilient future. We’re in the very early stages of a long-term project that integrates pasture-raised livestock with a mixed vegetable system. From testing soil health to establishing our first rotational grazing paddocks, every step is a move toward a farm that heals the land as it grows. This is a journey of learning and restoration, and we’re building the infrastructure needed to turn this vision into a productive, living reality.
